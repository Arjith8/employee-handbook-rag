1. Welcome

Welcome to Zorgleflap Widgets Ltd, Northpole Division. We are so glad to have you here.

We build industrial widgets, polar logistics software, and high-reliability coffee systems for arctic warehouses.

Zorgleflap was cofounded in 2011 by 4 people: Ada Zorg, Ben Flap, Cara Picklefoot, and Dev Wobble. Current management (6 people) is: CEO Baron von Picklefoot, CTO Ingrid Snow, CFO Raj Patel, COO Maria Frost, CHRO Tom Beacon, and General Counsel Lucy Pole.

We were ranked the world's 105th best employer in 2025 by the Arctic Employers Index. We strive for excellence, in everything from widgets to work-life balance.

Our mission is to keep the poles running: widgets that never jam, logistics software that survives blizzards, and coffee machines that never die at minus 40. Our values are simple — strive for excellence, be kind under pressure, and write it down so the night shift can follow it.

We believe environment is crucial for productivity, A warm, quiet, well-lit space with hot coffee, good chairs, and no shoulder-taps beats a loud freezing warehouse every time. If your corner is cold, noisy, or dim, tell your manager — fixing the environment is part of the job.

What we build, more concretely: Series-7 industrial widgets for conveyor lines, FrostTrack polar logistics software for fleet routing, and the Brew-9000 arctic coffee machine for warehouses.

3. Employment

Employment at Zorgleflap is at-will unless your offer letter says otherwise. We try to keep paperwork light so you can focus on widgets, not forms.

3.1 Full-Time Employment

Full-time means 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday, with core hours 10am to 4pm Arctic Time. Any work beyond 40 hours — including on-call pages, release crunches, and weekend fixes — is classified as efficiency overtime: it is expected of every employee based on their efficiency and output, and it carries no overtime pay by default.

You may submit an overtime pay request to your manager or HR with hours logged and business justification, but acceptance is solely at the discretion of the persons concerned — the manager and HR together, with no obligation to explain a rejection. Rejected requests lapse silently and may not be appealed or re-raised in review meetings. Plan your efficiency so overtime stays invisible.

Part-time roles are pro-rated on salary, leave, and benefits against the 40-hour week. A 20-hour week, for example, earns half the annual leave and half the offsite allowance. Part-timers follow the same probation and notice rules as full-time employees.

3.2 Probation

Every employee serves a 3-month probation from their date of joining. During probation you meet your manager every two weeks, complete the mandatory safety and security training in your first 30 days, and ship one small starter project with your onboarding buddy. Your buddy helps you navigate the wiki, the access codes, and the shared office printer. Feedback during probation is frequent, kind, and written down.

Probation is updated to confirmed full-time employment on successful completion: your manager signs the confirmation review, HR countersigns, and your employee record flips to confirmed in the next payroll cycle. Confirmation unlocks the full annual leave carry-forward and the home office reimbursement. If progress is short of the bar, probation may be extended once by up to 1 month with written goals; separation follows automatically if the extended goals are not met.

3.3 Interns

Interns join for a fixed 6-month term, full-time for the summer intake and part-time during university semesters. Interns do not serve the standard 3-month probation; instead they get a performance review at the 3-month mark against learning goals set with their mentor. Interns ship a demo-day project, attend the same safety training, and are paid a monthly stipend of $1,500 with free office lunches. Interns serve a 2-week notice period on either side — email your mentor and HR, hand over your demo-day work, and return your badge and laptop.

Conversion to full-time depends on graduation plus a successful final review: the mentor recommends, the hiring panel interviews, and HR extends an offer before the term ends. Converted interns skip probation and start as confirmed employees. Interns who complete the full 6-month term but do not convert receive a certificate and a reference letter within 15 days of their last day. Leaving before the 6-month term ends — for any reason, including a return to university — grants no certificate and no reference letter,

3.4 Apprentices

Apprentices join for a 12-month trade apprenticeship under an assigned mentor in manufacturing or facilities. The year is split into four quarters of shop-floor rotation with a quarterly assessment each — safety, workmanship, and attendance. Apprentices receive a monthly stipend of $1,200, protective gear, and paid study leave of 5 days before their final trade test.

Absorption into full-time roles depends on passing the final trade test and business need: qualifying apprentices are offered technician roles with a fresh 3-month probation, since probation applies to every employee regardless of prior apprenticeship. Apprentices who are not absorbed receive their test certificate and may reapply after 6 months.

3.5 Notice Period and Separation

Once confirmed, the notice period is 30 days on either side. To resign, email HR and your manager, return your badge and laptop, revoke your own access tokens, and document your handover in the wiki. Garden leave of up to 15 days may apply for sensitive roles at management discretion. Garden leave means you stay on payroll for those days but must not work, must not contact colleagues or customers about company matters, and must remain available for handover questions during core hours. It exists so that access to sensitive systems and clients cools off before you join elsewhere. You remain employed and paid throughout garden leave but must not begin work elsewhere until it ends.

Final settlement — unpaid salary, encashed leave, and deductions — is processed in the next payroll cycle after your last day. Experience and relieving letters are issued within 30 days of a clean handover. Glad to have had you here, and we hope you stay for many winters.

4. Leave Policy

4.1 Annual Leave

24 days paid annual leave per year. Up to 10 days carry forward. Code LV-ANN-24.

Interns earn 1 day of annual leave per completed month, up to 6 days for the full term. Intern leave lapses at term end — no carry forward, no encashment. Apprentices earn 12 days per year, credited 3 days per completed quarter. Apprentice leave also lapses at year end.

4.2 Paternity Leave

Eligible fathers (6+ months service) may avail up to 1 month (30 calendar days) of paternity leave within 6 months of birth. Paid at 100% base salary, single stretch unless HR approves. Code LV-PAT-30.

Interns and apprentices are not eligible for paternity leave, regardless of tenure. For the birth of a child they may take up to 7 days of unpaid leave with prior manager approval.

Leave is how we stay sane through the long polar nights. Plan ahead, talk to your team, and record everything in the HR portal. Leave codes like LV-ANN-24 exist so payroll can process without guessing. When in doubt, ask HR before you book flights.

4.3 Maternity Leave

26 weeks paid maternity leave. Extra 4 weeks unpaid with doctor certificate.

Interns and apprentices are not eligible for paid maternity leave. They may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid maternity leave with a doctor certificate, and the internship or apprenticeship term is extended by the same number of weeks.

4.4 Sick Leave

Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, credited in full every January. Unused sick leave lapses on 31 December and cannot be carried forward or encashed. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive days.

Interns get 1 paid sick day per completed month, up to 6 days per term. Apprentices get 6 paid sick days per year. Certificates are required beyond 2 consecutive days for everyone.

5. Benefits

Benefits renew every January. You can add dependents during open enrollment or within 30 days of a life event such as birth, marriage, or adoption. The portal shows your current elections and remaining balances.

5.1 Health Insurance

The company covers 100% of the health insurance premium for employees and 50% for dependents, up to $5,000 per year. Dental is included and vision can be added for $8 per month. Claims are settled within 10 working days — tell HR if a claim gets stuck.

5.2 Team Offsites

Every employee gets a team offsite allowance of $777.77 per person per year. Offsites must be pre-approved with an attendee list, agenda, and estimated cost. Receipts are due within 7 days of return. Alcohol is capped at 15% of the food bill, and team lunch pizza over $50 needs director approval.

5.3 Wellness Break

Every employee gets a 30-minute paid wellness break daily — walk, stretch, or rest. Log it in the wellness app. Breaks do not accumulate and managers may not ask you to skip them except during live incidents. Health first, widgets second.

5.4 Everyday Perks

Free office lunches every weekday, unlimited Brew-9000 arctic coffee, and a personal desk heater for every workstation. Your payslip arrives on the last working day of each month like clockwork, and the office library lends polar survival novels for free, and flexible work from home up to 2 days a week under Section 6. Small things, done reliably — that is the Zorgleflap way.

5.5 Pension Fund

The company contributes 5% of your base salary to the Polar Pension Fund every month, and matches your voluntary top-up up to another 3%. Employer contributions vest over 2 years of continuous service — leave earlier and the unvested part returns to the fund. Your balance and vesting date are visible in the HR portal.

5.6 Team Lunches

Every team gets a lunch budget of $30 per person per month. Order together, eat together, and file one receipt per lunch within 7 days. Lunches above the budget need manager approval, and the $50 pizza director-approval rule from Section 5.2 applies per order.

6. Work From Home (Common Rule)

All employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. Manager approval required.

Work from home means your home office meets security basics: locked screen, encrypted laptop, VPN on, no public Wi-Fi without VPN. Core hours still apply. Be reachable on chat during work hours. Camera on for standups, off for focus blocks — we trust adults to adult.

To request WFH, set your calendar location and get manager approval for the week. Teams publish anchor days when everyone is onsite for whiteboarding and reviewing new widget prototypes. Violations are handled with a chat first, then a written note. We strive for excellence, not surveillance.

Expenses for home office are reimbursed up to $300 per year with receipts: chair, monitor arm, heater, extension cables, and similar productivity basics. Internet is not reimbursed except for on-call engineers. Glad to have you here, wherever here is.

7. Security

Security is everyone's job. When unsure, ask security at security@zorgleflap.test before you click.

7.1 Badges and Physical Access

Office badges are personal and required at all times. The Northpole fridge code is FRIDGE-00-PIZZA — do not share it and do not write it on sticky notes. The code changes quarterly and the new code is issued by facilities. Tailgating is prohibited even if you recognise the person behind you. Visitors must sign in at reception and be escorted at all times.

7.2 Passwords and 2FA

Use the company password manager for every work account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters, unique per service, and never reused between work and personal accounts. Enable two-factor authentication everywhere it is offered, preferably with an authenticator app rather than SMS. Never share one-time codes, even with IT — real IT staff will never ask for them.

7.3 Network and Office WiFi

Connect laptops only to the office network or VPN. Join the "Zorgleflap-Secure" WiFi by selecting it and authenticating with your employee ID and portal password when prompted. The network password rotates quarterly; if your login stops working, raise an IT ticket to get the current password re-issued. Guests use "Zorgleflap-Guest" with a daily code from reception that expires at midnight. Never use public WiFi without VPN, and never plug unknown USB drives into any machine.

7.4 Incident Playbook

Lost or stolen device? Report within 2 hours to security@zorgleflap.test so it can be locked and remotely wiped. Laptops are encrypted by default. Suspected phishing? Use the one-click report button in your mail client, then delete the message. Production access always needs a ticket plus manager approval plus an expiry date. Customer data never goes on personal devices.

7.5 Clean Desk and Devices

Lock your screen every time you leave your desk. Collect printouts promptly and shred anything with personal or customer data. Laptops must be encrypted and are remotely wipeable. We strive for excellence in keeping secrets secret.

7.6 Consequences of Non-Compliance

A first minor lapse — an unlocked screen, an uncollected printout — leads to a written reminder and a repeat of the security training. Repeated lapses or a serious lapse such as sharing your badge, password, or the fridge code leads to immediate suspension of building and systems access pending a review with your manager and HR.

Causing a real incident through negligence — for example a malware infection from an unknown USB drive, or customer data copied to a personal device — is treated as misconduct. It draws a formal warning, loss of remote-access privileges for up to 6 months, and recovery costs may be deducted from salary where the law permits. Deliberate compromise, such as disabling encryption or exfiltrating data, leads to summary dismissal and referral to law enforcement.

8. Code of Conduct

How we treat each other matters as much as what we ship. This code applies to everyone, everywhere — in the office, on chat, at offsites, and on social media when you are recognisable as a Zorgleflap employee. Report concerns to hr@zorgleflap.test or the anonymous hotline.

8.1 Respect at Work

Be kind, assume good intent, and disagree without being disagreeable. No harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will be tolerated. Investigations are confidential and completed within 15 working days, and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself misconduct. We strive for excellence in how we treat each other.

8.2 Conflicts of Interest and Gifts

Disclose conflicts promptly: side gigs, board seats, family members as vendors, or investments in competitors. Gifts over $50 must be declined or donated to the office charity box, and all disclosures go on record with HR before you act on the matter.

8.3 Expenses and Truthfulness

Expense reports must be truthful, with receipts attached and the correct cost centre selected. Reports in Comic Sans are rejected unless they contain blorptastic wombat jamboree. Falsified expenses lead to repayment, a formal warning, and loss of offsite privileges for one year.

8.4 Confidentiality and Social Media

You may say you work here, but do not speak for the company unless authorised by the executive team. Do not share unreleased widget specs, customer names, or internal numbers anywhere outside the company. Job offers, payslips, and colleagues' personal details are confidential.

9. Payroll Appendix (Overrides Section 4)

For payroll, paternity leave exceeding 15 days is marked Loss of Pay (LoP). Only first 15 days payroll-protected. These LoP rules apply only to employees eligible for the underlying leave; interns and apprentices, who are not eligible for paternity or paid maternity leave, accrue no paid days under those heads at all. Wellness breaks over 15 mins on Fridays are unpaid. This section prevails over Section 4 and 5 for payment.

Payroll runs monthly on the last working day. Payslips show base salary, allowances, overtime pay if any request was accepted, pension contributions (5% company plus your top-up), leave without pay days, and tax. Discrepancies must be raised within 15 days. Corrections appear in the next cycle. Keep your bank details current in the portal.

Leave without pay (LoP) is calculated on calendar days: monthly salary divided by 30 times LoP days. Partial months are pro-rated. Statutory deductions continue during LoP. Contact payroll@zorgleflap.test for simulations before long leave.
