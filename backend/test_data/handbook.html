2. Employment Basics

Full-time is 40 hours/week. Probation is 3 months. Notice period is 30 days.

Employment at Zorgleflap is at-will unless your offer letter says otherwise. Full-time means 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday, with core hours 10am to 4pm Arctic Time. Part-time roles are pro-rated. Interns are full-time for the summer term and follow the same probation rules. Whatever whatever — we try to keep paperwork light so you can focus on widgets, not forms.

Probation lasts 3 months. During probation you meet your manager biweekly, complete mandatory safety training, and ship one small starter project. Your buddy from onboarding helps you navigate the wiki, the fridge code, and the mysterious third floor printer. We strive for excellence, so feedback is frequent, kind, and written down.

Notice period is 30 days once confirmed. To resign, email HR and your manager, return your badge and laptop, and document your handover. Garden leave may apply for sensitive roles. Final settlement is processed in the next payroll cycle. Glad to have you here, and we hope you stay for many winters.

5. Work From Home (Common Rule)

All employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. Manager approval required.

Work from home means your home office meets security basics: locked screen, encrypted laptop, VPN on, no public Wi-Fi without VPN. Core hours still apply. Be reachable on chat during work hours. Camera on for standups, off for focus blocks — we trust adults to adult.

To request WFH, set your calendar location and get manager approval for the week. Teams publish anchor days when everyone is onsite for whiteboarding and taste-testing new widget prototypes. Violations are handled with a chat first, then a written note. We strive for excellence, not surveillance.

For payroll, paternity leave exceeding 15 days is marked Loss of Pay (LoP). Only first 15 days payroll-protected. Wellness breaks over 15 mins on Fridays are unpaid. This section prevails over Section 3 and 4 for payment.

Payroll runs monthly on the last working day. Payslips show base, allowances, deductions, leave without pay days, and tax. Discrepancies must be raised within 15 days. Corrections appear in the next cycle. Whatever whatever — keep your bank details current in the portal.

Leave without pay (LoP) is calculated on calendar days: monthly salary divided by 30 times LoP days. Partial months are pro-rated. Statutory deductions continue during LoP. Contact payroll@zorgleflap.test for simulations before long leave.
